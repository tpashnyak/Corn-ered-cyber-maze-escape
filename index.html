Corn-ered!
The Cyber Maze Escape

Malcaw the hacker crow has locked down the Byte Acres corn maze. Crack seven cyber gates, collect the letters of the exit password, and get out before he eats your data.

It goes on your badge. First name or initials only. Never put your full name online when you don't have to!

Before you go in

  • 🔊Turn your sound on. Kernel and Malcaw talk to you. Captions are always on screen.
  • 🔦Each gate you crack drops one letter of the exit password.
  • 🌽Stuck? Press Ask Kernel for a hint, or Read aloud to hear the question. Wrong answers just let you try again. Nothing is timed.
  • 🏅Escape to earn your Maze Defender badge.
